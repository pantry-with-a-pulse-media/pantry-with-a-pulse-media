Pantry With a Pulse Privacy Policy

Pantry With a Pulse creates and shares educational heart-smart food content on social media.

We do not sell personal information. If someone contacts Pantry With a Pulse by email or through a social platform, we may use that information only to respond to the message, manage the account, or improve our content.

Our automation tools may use authorized social media APIs to publish Pantry With a Pulse videos to accounts that we own or manage. OAuth tokens and private credentials are stored securely and are not published in this repository.

Social platforms may collect their own analytics, cookies, and account data under their own privacy policies.

For questions, contact: pantrywithapulse@gmail.com
